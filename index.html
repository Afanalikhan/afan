About Me

Hello! I'm Afan Ali Khan, a young programmer, founder, and innovator from the beautiful mountains of Chitral, Pakistan. My journey into technology began when I was just 11 years old. Since then I have dedicated myself to learning programming, web development, problem solving and building projects that make a positive impact. I completed Harvard University's CS50x Introduction to Computer Science and continue exploring new technologies every day. Beyond coding, I actively participate in scouting, community service, volunteer work, leadership activities and international youth initiatives. My mission is to use technology to connect communities, preserve culture and create opportunities for people around the world.
